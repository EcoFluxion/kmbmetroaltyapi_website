Who We Are

KMB was established by Troy company, operating in Turkey since 1996, and Kyivmetrobud company, operating in Ukraine and many other countries since 1949.

KMB specializes in the design and construction of infrastructure (railway, metro, airport, tunnel, sewerage system, etc.), power plants and dams, public buildings, etc.

KMB's competitive advantage is based on high engineering power, experience, high quality and excellent solution proposals. These elements have made the company a preferred contractor by national and international investors.
